⚙️ Mathematical Models (Eq. 1 - 31)

Solar PV Power (Eq. 1-3):
P_pv(t) = P_pv,r · (G(t)/1000) · [1 + α_c·(T_amb + 0.0256·G(t) - 25)]
Biogas Generator Power (Eq. 4):
P_B(t) = (V_B(t) · CV_B · η_B) / (860 · t_B)
Pumped Hydro Generation / Pumping (Eq. 6-8):
E_gen,PHES = min[ V(t-1)/3600, Q_T ] · η_T · η_P · ρ · g · H_net
Fast SMES Superconducting Buffer (Eq. 12-15):
E_SMES(t) = 0.5 · L_SMES · I_SMES² (Sub-second transient response)
Multi-Objective Pareto Functions (Eq. 29-31):
F1 = min{Total NPC (₱)} | F2 = min{LPSP} | F3 = min{CO₂ Emissions}

🔄 Operating Dispatch Modes (Section 4.2)

  • Mode I (P_pv > P_L): High solar surplus. SMES charges within milliseconds, PHES pumps water to upper reservoir.
  • Mode II (0 < P_pv < P_L): PV deficit. SMES absorbs rapid step changes while PHES hydro turbine ramps to meet deficit.
  • Mode III (P_pv > P_L, Storage Full): Excess energy exported to national grid under feed-in-tariff.
  • Mode IV (P_pv = 0): Night / zero solar. Biogas generator starts up and pairs with PHES/SMES to supply base load.
  • Mode V (P_gen < P_L): Storage depleted or extreme peak. Backed up by national distribution grid import.
